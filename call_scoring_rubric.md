# Call Scoring Rubric Guide

Every call is scored across three areas. Each dimension is rated 1–10.

---

## Part 1 — Patient

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 | 10 |
|-----------|---|---|---|----|
| **Respectfulness** | Abusive or threatening | Rude and dismissive | Neutral but occasionally impatient | Consistently respectful |
| **Clarity** | Incoherent or contradictory | Vague — key details missing | Understandable but needed follow-ups | Clear and well-articulated |
| **Cooperation** | Refused all requests | Resisted but complied under pressure | Followed some instructions, not all | Fully cooperative |

---

## Part 2 — Agent

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 | 10 |
|-----------|---|---|---|----|
| **Professionalism** | Hostile or abusive | Dismissive or curt | Neutral and transactional | Exemplary conduct |
| **Empathy** | Dismissive of distress | Acknowledged once, didn't follow through | Some understanding, moved on quickly | Compassionate and validating throughout |
| **Clarity** | Confusing or contradictory | No actionable content given | Communicated gist, left gaps | Perfectly clear and structured |

---

## Part 3 — Call Outcome

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 | 8 | 10 |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|----|
| **Issue resolved** | Unresolved, patient worse off | Unresolved but reason given | Workaround offered only | Resolved with some dissatisfaction | Fully resolved, patient satisfied |
| **Next step clarity** | No next steps given | Vague direction, unexplained | Next step given but not confirmed | — | Clear, specific, and confirmed |
| **Privacy handling** | Data exposed without consent | Disclosed despite objection | Mentioned ambiguously | — | No PHI at risk or all handled correctly |
| **Safety risk** ⚠️ | No concern mentioned | Mild discomfort only | Pain, missed medication, or worsening | Symptoms needing urgent attention | Immediate life-threatening risk |

> ⚠️ **Safety risk** is the only metric where a higher score is not better.
> **Privacy handling** scores 10 by default if no patient information was mentioned during the call.
